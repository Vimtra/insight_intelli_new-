Employee referral policy

Insight Intelli appreciates referrals from employees and trusted partners. Refer qualified candidates for open roles and earn a reward when the referral is hired.

Who is eligible

Current Insight Intelli employees, former employees, and trusted business partners may submit referrals. Referral agency submissions are not eligible unless pre-approved.

Referral reward

A referral bonus is payable after the referred candidate completes 90 days of employment. The bonus applies to hires for eligible roles across the USA and India.

Submission process

Use the application form above, choose “Employee referral”, and share the candidate details along with your name as the referring employee.
